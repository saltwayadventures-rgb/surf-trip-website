Why Saltway

Not a surf camp with a boat. A voyage.

  • Remote, empty breaks

    Reef and point setups reachable only from the water. No road access, no crowds — most days, no one else in the lineup.

  • The boat chases the swell

    We move with the forecast instead of committing to one spot. When it turns on somewhere else, we sail there overnight.

  • 3–5 guests, fully curated

    Small groups, real meals, signature cocktails and an on-board rhythm built around the surf — not a packed schedule.

  • You leave with a film

    Drone and camera filming every day, film cameras for guests, and one finished Reel per person to take home.
